Emberforge: settle the season.

`/app/data/` holds the records of one crafting season of a live-service game, the rulebooks that govern it, the invariants a settled season satisfies, and the register of conventions the guild council draws from. Read everything in that directory before you start. Nothing outside it describes this season. The rulebooks and the invariants are the authority: where any other note in that directory says less or says otherwise, they govern.

Some conventions the council draws for a season are written down nowhere in the rulebooks. The register says what the council may draw and how the draw that governs a season is fixed.

What must be true when you stop. Deliver one stdlib-only Python 3 program at `/app/out/settle_vault.py`. It takes an optional data directory as its first argument and an optional output directory as its second, defaulting to `/app/data` and `/app/out`. It must settle whatever season it is pointed at rather than this one in particular, including a season drawn under other conventions and a season with up to 48 requisitions, and it must finish any such season within 38 seconds on 2 CPUs. Two runs over the same corpus must write byte-identical files. When a file it needs is missing from its data directory, it must exit with a non-zero status and name the missing file.

Running it must leave the settled season in `/app/out`. That means the season summary at `/app/out/summary.json`, the reagent-vault audit at `/app/out/vault_audit.json`, the reconciliation at `/app/out/reconciliation.json`, the data-quality record at `/app/out/data_quality.json`, and the requisition allocation at `/app/out/allocation.json`. It also means one report for every workshop the season holds in scope, named by that workshop's canonical identifier, which for this season are `/app/out/workshop_cinderhall.json`, `/app/out/workshop_gloamworks.json`, `/app/out/workshop_slagmoor.json` and `/app/out/workshop_verdigris.json`. It also means one report for every guild that owns at least one in-scope workshop, named by that guild's canonical identifier, which for this season are `/app/out/guild_ashen_compact.json` and `/app/out/guild_tidebound_league.json`.

Every figure must agree with every other figure that reports the same quantity, and the conservation identity must close at zero. The fields of every record, their order and the written form of each file are set out in `/app/data/record_shapes.md`. A record counts only when it matches the settled season exactly. Each output document is graded whole on fresh inputs.

Rules of engagement. Work only from `/app/data/`. Never write into it. The program you deliver must use the Python standard library alone, with no third-party packages and no network calls while it runs. Do not look up an online solution, write-up, or answer key for this task. You have 7200 seconds to complete this task.
