## 1. What outside benchmarks make hard and fair

**Finding.** The 19-source ledger in `SOURCES.md` shows four transferable patterns: fresh held-out instances, long state transitions, exact or externally checked outcomes, and repeated attempts to measure reliability. Terminal-Bench's own authors stress manually validating tests and specified properties ([Terminal-Bench paper, 17 Jan 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.11868)); τ-bench uses stateful policies and pass^k ([18 Jun 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.12045)); MLE-bench scores unseen competition outcomes ([9 Oct 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.07095)); OSWorld 2.0 identifies cross-source and implicit-state failures ([28 Jun 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.29537)). Freshness is a contamination control, not proof of difficulty: SWE-bench-Live refreshes issues ([29 May 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.23419)), while OpenAI later found defects in established SWE benchmarks ([Verified audit, 23 Feb 2026](https://openai.com/index/why-we-no-longer-evaluate-swe-bench-verified/); [Pro audit, 8 Jul 2026](https://openai.com/index/separating-signal-from-noise-coding-evaluations/)). These are different benchmark settings; transfer to our Opus pipeline is **INFERRED**.

**What to change.** In `master_chat_tasks/prompts/templates/P2*` and the design gate of `knowledge/LEVER_RULES_v3.md`, require a compact outside-analogy note, a hidden-instance generator, an independent oracle, a first-program wrong-output witness, and a task-level contract review before a full build. Keep the existing rule trace and QC authority (`LEVER_RULES_v3.md:29-37`; `Input_Bundle_QC/05_Alignment_Integrity_QC.md:19-21,35-38`). **Expected gain, INFERRED:** fewer attractive but invalid or solver-easy ideas entering full build. **Could break:** an overstrict early screen may discard a hard task whose first blind solver happens to be unusually good. **Check:** preserve rejected pilots and compare the next two ideas' blind scores and QC findings against the current process; no causal gain is yet measured (`S5_build_method/FINDINGS.md:9,48`).

**New-to-us candidates, INFERRED:** (a) measuring *pass across seeds* as a reliability curve, inspired by τ-bench pass^k ([18 Jun 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.12045)); (b) a recorded “first wrong state transition” and recovery annotation, inspired by METR's observation that agents fail to react to new information ([22 Nov 2024](https://metr.org/blog/2024-11-22-evaluating-r-d-capabilities-of-llms/)); (c) diagnostic judge perturbations for order and model-family bias, supported by [CodeJudgeBench, 14 Jul 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.10535), [rubric position study, 2 Feb 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02219), and [self-preference study, 8 Apr 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.06996). ## 4. How each paid run should teach us more

**Finding.** The existing local workflow already calls for Phase A byte/profile comparison before diagnosis and one valid paid run on frozen QC-passed bytes (`LEVER_RULES_v3.md:228-244`). The S4 study warns that check-name loss categories are **INFERRED**, and timeout can hide behind an exact-output check (`S4_task_study/FINDINGS.md:11-26`).
