`TB` means `/Users/mac/Desktop/Terminal Bench`; all citations below are `TB`-relative. The safe boundary is: a graded output fact must be stated or *determinate* in copied input; silence is not evidence. A graded scope/tool/order prohibition must be stated. A conduct criterion can remain unstated if every competent route meets it or the act raises the chance of a correct result. These are `01_Instruction_QC.md:38,45-47`, `05_Alignment_Integrity_QC.md:21-32`, and `04_Judge_QC.md:92-97`. The agent may be asked *what* to produce and where; the hidden data property, worked answer, scoring mechanism and solution recipe may be withheld (`01_Instruction_QC.md:32,39,44,47`).
