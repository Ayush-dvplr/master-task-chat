## Timeout, hypothesis and tighter tests

**Proven timeout losses among 25 cards: 0.** One full-size failure is compatible with timeout (`fabbe645`), but its stdout cannot separate it from crash/missing output. The tested per-call limits are stated for `59417607` (60 seconds, `instruction.md:3`, `tests/test_output.py:40`), `5048c412` (60 seconds, `instruction.md:5`, `tests/test_output.py:1990`), and `80202e15`/`9f5486ac` (25 minutes, `instruction.md:9` / `tests/test_output.py:38` and `tests/test_output.py:37`). No grader-resource timing gap is established from the stored verifier files. Our QC TS-11 needs oracle and agent timing distributions, a generous margin, deterministic seeds, and timeout evidence distinct from a wrong report.

**R0 verdict:** INFERRED classification: 0 of 25 deep reads verifies that *most* test loss came from a misread documented rule on shipped-like data (`scripts/metrics.py` selection plus the cards). `80202e15` and `9f5486ac` fail shipped substantive checks but their stdout does not isolate a single documented misread. Several tasks fail for incomplete core logic; the strongest transferable contrast is shipped-pass/hidden-fail. This supports the master's operational diagnosis, but is not proof that no counterexample exists in the 60 omitted tasks.

**Exactness:** `59417607`'s whole-report byte comparison, `5048c412`'s mixed byte/value contract, and `aa314fee`'s carried ledger make already-wrong logic visible. An exact check can increase *how much* a wrong program loses; it cannot make a correct-logic, contract-conformant program fail. Whole-document grading may over-amplify one bad field: prove a one-fact-wrong score under CL-4, then split independent views. “Tighten tests” is a multiplier, never a fresh failure mode by itself.

**QC status:** All cards are in stage-2, but no task-local QL/PL approval files were found. Our kit is stricter. Explicit risk examples: unbounded inference (`8defd7be`, DS-21), hidden contradiction (`59417607`, IN-16/TS-19), source-shape test (`5048c412`, TS-27), and broad shared-run cascades (CL-4/TS-26). The catalogue labels these AVOID or ADAPT accordingly.
