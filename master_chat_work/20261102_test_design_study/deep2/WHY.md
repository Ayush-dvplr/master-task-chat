1. **Actual program mismatch plus a firing witness**, not a clever description, dominates. Shipped-fitted parsers are concrete: 1d61 missing cell refs (`run_levelnet.py:456-470` versus `solve.py:213-236`); fab visible carrier literals (`optimize.py:605-615,639-645,731-733`); hangar's proposed row wrapper variant (`DESIGN.md:21-38`). Depot's exact a2 has no such witnessed gap in 2,312 cases (`A2_SCORE.md:4-33`).
2. **Shipped examples underexercise documented generality.** a710's sealed shipped components versus unsealed hidden (`test_output.py:106-125`), fab's variable codes and endianness (`:186-212`), 18b's sparse rate cards (`rate_card.md:8-12`), and our hangar's plain Word rows versus standard wrappers (`DESIGN.md:18-38`) all fit this pattern. Slipway's explicit warning `instruction.md:19` did not prevent an exact paid program; its r2 adds a specific unseen crosswalk placement (`HARDEN_REPORT.md:29-42`).
3. **Weight amplification makes one mistake look like a broad failure.** 802's MET FX field suffices for all 31 source-slice failures =155 points (`test_output.py:5769-5823`), while 37's all-corpus gate loses 64 without locating a first error (`:3812-3839`). Depot also has whole-document checks but scored 557/557 because the program is correct (`A2_SCORE.md:4-25`): comparison style explains the size of loss after a bug, not the bug itself.
4. **Hard scale ceilings matter when published.** a710 step 46 needed 95.6 s on an unsealed copy under a stated 60 s bound (`instruction.md:5`, `test_output.py:1191-1201`). We lack retained per-hidden times and oracle margin, so treat its +840 as strongly inferred, not a measured timing breakdown.
