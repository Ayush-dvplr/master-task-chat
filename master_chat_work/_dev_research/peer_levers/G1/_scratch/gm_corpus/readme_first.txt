This directory is the complete export set and written contract for the
quarterly arena-ladder season settlement. A prior settlement pipeline
produced figures that did not tie out and the settlement was rejected;
your job is to recompute the corrected settlement, not to recover a format.
Filenames are deliberately generic; read the contents to learn each file's
role. Start from the pipeline contract overview and the per-stage
specification files. Every rule you need is written down somewhere under
this directory. Three load-bearing figures live only inside the spreadsheet,
the policy PDF, and the levy addendum DOCX.
