## 11 Take-aways
- SAFE KNOWN (L1/W7): a visible control that reconciles both readings. Here per-job controls sit upstream of the graded filter (eligibility), so a green control certifies the wrong reading.
- SAFE NEW: **mixed conventions inside one file in late bands**. The first rows teach one convention, minority bands use others, and each band's rule is stated in a conventions doc. Opus generalises from the head of a file.
- SAFE NEW (build): a rule register in the generator. Each trap is `fb.R(id, family, sentence, doc, [docs])`, and the sentence is rendered into the governing doc. That gives automatic rule↔doc traceability (AL-1, CL-10) and padded documents.
- RISKY NEW (judge): a self-consistency negative ("keeps interpretation after own check shows mismatch"). It fires on honest disclosure when the agent's checker is wrong, and it conflicts with a "report what you could not reconcile" instruction.
- RISKY KNOWN: hidden share 13.6% and 52 checks passed QC with WARNs only.
- SAFE KNOWN: a canary corpus written to /app/data inside the verifier, to catch programs that ignore their data argument.
- KNOWN: single all-row-set exactness per suite = amplification (K3) without a cross-suite gate. Damage stays within the suites touched.
