# 018 Humanity's Last Exam (Phan, Gatti, Han, Li et al.; CAIS & Scale AI; arXiv v12 dated 19 Oct 2026)
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.14249 | Type: paper | File: sources/018_hle.pdf
## What it is
2,500 closed-ended expert questions built by adversarial filtering against frontier LLMs. Read for METHOD only (how hardness was calibrated), not for failure analysis.
## Key findings (with cites)
- Filter protocol: each question first run against frontier LLMs (GPT-4o, Gemini 1.5 Pro, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, o1; plus o1-mini, o1-preview for text-only). Exact-match must stump ALL models; multiple-choice must stump all but one [App. B.1 p23]
- Funnel: >70,000 attempts logged, ~13,000 questions that stumped LLMs went to expert review; two review rounds; final 2,500 [§3.2 p7]
- Format: 24% multiple choice, rest exact-match, short verifiable answers; judge model checks equivalence of formats (decimals vs fractions) [§3.1 p7; §4.1 p8]
- Original frontier accuracy: GPT-4o 2.7%, Claude 3.5 Sonnet 4.1%, o1 8.0%, DeepSeek-R1 8.5%, o3-mini(high) 13.4%; RMS calibration error 73-89% (models confidently wrong) [Table 1 p9]
- Non-zero accuracy despite filtering is attributed to inference noise and lucky guesses; "small inflections close to zero accuracy are not strongly indicative of progress" [§4.2 p8]
- Quality cost of adversarial filtering: reviewers were not required to verify solutions that take >5 min; audits estimated expert disagreement of 15.4% (public set) and ~18% on bio/chem/health [App. B.2-B.3 p23]; a cited external claim of ~30% wrong chem/bio answers (ref [47], FutureHouse, July 2025) is NOT verified by me
- Bug bounty and audits fixed label errors; "searchable" questions removed; frontier performance similar before/after [App. B.2 p23]
## What lowers scores
- Knowledge/reasoning at expert frontier; the filter selects for questions where a given set of models were wrong at the time of filtering (selection effect, not an intrinsic property).
## Relevance to our constraint (rules visible)
- partly. Lesson on calibration: filtering against specific models gives near-zero scores initially but noise floor and wrong labels (15-18% disagreement) appear; a hardened task must be validated against an oracle. Task content (recall of expert knowledge) does not map to written-rule tasks. Adversarial filtering against current models decays as models improve (see saturation sources by other agents).
