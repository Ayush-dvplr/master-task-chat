# The calibration slice

Alongside the season proper, this corpus carries a second, smaller season that
the guild council has already settled and published. Its records are the ones
whose names begin `calibration_`. They have the same shape as the records of the
season proper and they obey the same rulebooks.

The four conventions the council draws for a season - the return schedule, the
reduction convention, the operator order and the scarcity reference - are drawn
once for a whole council year and govern every season the council opens in that
year. The calibration slice and the season proper belong to the same council
year, so the draw that settled the slice is the draw that settles the season
proper.

The council publishes two things from the slice's settlement:

- the slice's own season summary, published as the calibration controls in
  `calibration_controls.json`;
- for one workshop of the slice, the units of each reagent that its credited
  salvages returned, published as the example report in
  `example_workshop_report.json`. Only the reagents that came back are listed.

Exactly one draw from the register reproduces both. No other draw reproduces
them. Nothing else the slice settled is published.
