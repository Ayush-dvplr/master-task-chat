## 2. What the table says

1. Difficulty is decided on the first real run; everything we spend before it is a bet. 5 of 7 bets lost (row 1), at ~47 sessions.
2. The two traps that worked were computation traps on hidden inputs (row 15). No stated wording lever cost Opus a point in any P14 (emberforge, schema, sensor).
3. QC non-convergence is about 1/3 reviewer noise, 1/3 our own harden edits, 1/3 our own fixes (row 2). Two thirds is self-inflicted and preventable by ordering (prove, freeze, then QC) and diff-only rechecks.
4. Grader leak is closed (row 8). Harness throughput is not the limit (row 11); policy (dec:25, dec:55) is.
5. The master's records contradict each other on the acceptance bar (row 12). Until one bar is written, "qc_clean" in status.py means nothing to PL.
