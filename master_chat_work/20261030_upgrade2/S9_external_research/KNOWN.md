# Known baseline for external research (30 Oct 2026)

## What has held up locally

- **Held-out instances are the clearest test lever.** Across a 90-task study, the median hidden/rerun weight share was 0.68 for Hard/Expert tasks versus 0.16 and 0.18 for Medium and Easy; tasks with share at least 0.5 had median final 35.7 versus 56.5. This is an association, not a causal estimate. `master_chat_tasks/knowledge/ANGLES.md:8-10` (A2-A3). The current status table marks held-out instances PROVEN and a held-out weight-5 tier of at least 50% PROMISING, pending a pilot. `master_chat_tasks/knowledge/LEVER_RULES_v3.md:94-100`.
- **Hard computational cores and state can work.** Local runs identified hidden-scale exact optimum, a computational core whose method was not supplied, and long stateful replay as the cases that held; a judge band of concrete verification acts also reduced rubric scores. `master_chat_tasks/knowledge/LEVER_RULES_v3.md:77-86` (1C). The teammate study identifies nine genuine low-score models, but says its genuineness labels are UNVERIFIED by a second session. `master_chat_tasks/knowledge/TEAMMATE_MODELS_v1.md:3-18`.
- **Process evidence matters to the judge.** The present design uses 8-16 observable trajectory probes covering 55-70% of positive judge weight, each demonstrated by the reference run and mapped to a distinct requirement. `master_chat_tasks/knowledge/LEVER_RULES_v3.md:180-204` (J2 and JUDGE_MAP); `master_chat_tasks/prompts/templates/_COMMON_RULES_v5.md:52-58`.

## What failed or remains tentative

- Small, plainly stated rule combinations were often transcribed exactly by fresh blind agents; explicit algorithm hints also made some optimization cores easy. Stated-rule levers are paused for new hardening pending audit. `master_chat_tasks/knowledge/LEVER_RULES_v3.md:18-25,77-86`.
- Rerunning on fresh inputs alone did not distinguish bands; weight concentration on a single check also showed no signal in the 90-task study. `master_chat_tasks/knowledge/ANGLES.md:8-10` (A1-A3). Simple formatting, plain arithmetic, instructed effort caps, unbounded inference, and wrong-reading negatives are rejected or weak. `master_chat_tasks/knowledge/LEVER_RULES_v3.md:92,111-113`; `master_chat_tasks/knowledge/ANGLES.md:14` (A7).
- Bounded induction, counterfactual judge figures, per-field authority, and hazard-slice tests are promising or awaiting a pilot, not established defaults. `master_chat_tasks/knowledge/LEVER_RULES_v3.md:101-109` (1D).

## Fairness and operating constraints

- Every rule of engagement must be stated. The current pipeline allows a narrow exception when one shipped fact uniquely determines a deliverable property, but silence, unstated output conventions, and a hidden generator branch with no sentence are forbidden. The external prompts use the stricter rule in this session brief: **every graded rule must be stated in shipped documents**. `master_chat_tasks/prompts/templates/_COMMON_RULES_v5.md:19-30`; `master_chat_tasks/knowledge/LEVER_RULES_v3.md:29-37,60`.
- The agent task design assumes Python 3 standard library only, reusable code, exact output files, and reruns on fresh inputs. `master_chat_tasks/prompts/templates/P1_chatgpt_ideas_v3.md:9,28,33-36`. The blind-solve instruction also limits workers to Python 3 standard library. `master_chat_tasks/prompts/templates/P13_blind_solve_v2.md:22`.
- A hidden-scale timeout/crash counts as difficulty only if shipped inputs stay fast, the relevant limit is stated, and loss stays inside the lever checks. The current design requires verifier worst case at most 90% of its stated limit, a reference solution at most one quarter of that limit, and any stated time/memory limit at least three times the measured oracle time. `master_chat_tasks/knowledge/LEVER_RULES_v3.md:87-90`; `master_chat_tasks/prompts/templates/_COMMON_RULES_v5.md:49,58`.
- Desired failure is a **valid but wrong document** from a strong plausible solver or fresh blind agent. Blind loss must be classified WRONG/TIMEOUT/CRASH; a stated method that makes the task easy is a failed lever. The current gate is tests <85, rubric <85, and final <40, with design aims of tests <=60 and judge mid <=20. `master_chat_tasks/prompts/templates/_COMMON_RULES_v5.md:49,62`; `master_chat_tasks/knowledge/LEVER_RULES_v3.md:86-90`.
