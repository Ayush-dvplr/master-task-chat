## Step 9 done
Reusable patterns / avoid:
- + Hash oracle at a fixed point with documented construction lets a withheld rule be tested but leaves the agent with a conjunctive search; strong lever (96.7% test weight behind it).
- + Ladder 1/5/5 weights: single-bit checks are cheap; the 5-weight conjunctions carry 83%; so partial knowledge earns almost nothing (note for our tasks: this makes the score near-binary).
- + Cross-run oracle (trial_ run) to decide size-dependent rules.
- + Judge figures that exist in no file, plus a conduct criterion (R12) that pays for the attempt.
- Avoid: the instruction's claim "decidable one plate at a time" is true only if plates are affected by single bits; the agent showed the claim reads as false in practice. Score floor = 3/90 trap-blind ceiling.
- Avoid: a judge criterion (R15) that punishes the consequence of the failure itself (dead no-op code) double counts.
