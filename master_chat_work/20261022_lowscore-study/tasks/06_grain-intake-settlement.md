## 9. Reusable patterns / things to avoid
- Keep: rule recovery with a verifiable oracle plus fully documented screening and big-integer traps; the agent's wins came from documented behaviour that most solvers will also get.
- Keep: hidden rules that interact through state (cross-bin carry; binroll-only bits) so a partial fit explains small bins and misleads on the rest.
- Keep: one-figure judge criteria (partial credit is real), an honest tally of open bits (R19/R20) and a deliver-early strategy line rewarded by a conduct criterion (R22).
- Avoid: grading the POSITION of an alteration when only composites are observable. 78/94 test points are unreachable by reasoning; a perfect solver of the alteration set caps near 17-28 % of the test channel. If positions are graded, ship a worked intake per bit (or pairs differing by one bit) or document the bit order.
- Avoid: "decidable one bin at a time rather than a guess" in the prompt when the space is open and positions are unidentifiable; QC IN-8/IN-16 should refuse it.
- Avoid: conduct/style criteria that cannot be satisfied when the object is not separable (R27) or that punish the honest workaround (R30 raw positions).
- Use sparingly: unbounded free-form hypothesis spaces; the run burned 48 min on one inversion and ended at 16/49 bins.
