ASHGROVE POINT REEFER YARD
dispatch admission ladder for AP-2044-D111

The ladder is read from the top. The first rung that catches a reefer
settles it and no lower rung is consulted for that reefer. This
printed order is the order of application. It is not the alphabetical
order of the rung codes.

rung 1  DR_UNKNOWN_PLUG
        the reefer names a plug the plug assignment register does not carry

rung 2  DR_FEEDER_OUT_OF_FORCE
        the plug hangs off a feeder the substation topology omits

rung 3  DR_WINDOW_EMPTY
        the connection window covers no interval of the grid

rung 4  DR_UNKNOWN_POWER_UNIT
        the nameplate names a power unit the unit register does not carry

rung 5  DR_PLUG_ZONE_REFUSED
        the plug zone refuses the temperature class the reefer declares

rung 6  DR_DUPLICATE_CONNECTION
        an earlier reefer in the same cluster already took the plug
