# Harbourline PM2.5 monitoring standard

This standard defines how the readings of the network become hourly values, daily values and
compliance findings. The fields of every input file are defined in the record dictionary
(`record_dictionary.md`); the reports are laid out as `report_layout.md` describes. Concentrations in
the reports are whole numbers of tenths of a microgram per cubic metre. The sections below are in
alphabetical order.

## Corrected concentration

The corrected concentration of a reading is its `pm25_raw` value multiplied by the unit's span factor
in force at the instant the reading represents. The product is taken exactly and then rounded half
up to a whole number of tenths.

## Daily value

A site's valid hours are grouped by the day of local standard time they lie in. When a day has at
least `day_min_hours` valid hours, it is a complete day and its daily value is the mean of the hourly
values of its valid hours, rounded down to a whole number of tenths. A day with at least one valid hour but fewer than
`day_min_hours` still has a daily value when the mean of the hourly values of its valid hours,
rounded down to a whole number of tenths, is greater than the daily standard; that mean is then its
daily value, and the day is not a complete day. Any other day has no daily value. A site's valid days are the days that have
a daily value.

## Daily standard

The daily standard is the policy's `daily_standard`, expressed in tenths of a microgram per cubic
metre.

## Design value

A site's daily values are ranked from the highest down. The design rank is one for a site with 1 to
50 daily values, two for 51 to 100, and one more for every further 50 daily values or part of 50.
The design value is the daily value at the design rank. A site with no daily value has neither.

## District and operator rollups

A district rollup gathers the sites of one reporting district, and an operator rollup gathers the
sites of one operator class. A rollup is in scope when at least one of its sites has a status other
than `incomplete`; every other rollup is out of scope. The status of a rollup in scope is
`nonattainment` when at least one of its sites is in nonattainment, and `attainment` otherwise. The
rolled-up valid readings of the network are the valid readings of the sites in the district rollups
that are in scope.

## Exceedance day

A day is an exceedance day of a site when its daily value is greater than the daily standard.

## Excluded readings

Every card row that is not a valid reading is an excluded reading. A row whose hour lies outside the
report window is counted as `outside_window` and under no other reason. Every other excluded row is
counted under each reason that applies to it: `undeployed` when it belongs to no site,
`missing_value` when its `pm25_raw` is empty, `flagged` when its `status` is not empty,
`uncalibrated` when its unit has no span factor in force at the instant it represents, and
`maintenance` when it belongs to a site and that instant lies inside a maintenance window of the
site.

## Hour of a reading

A reading counts toward the hour of local standard time that contains the instant the reading
represents. Which instant a reading represents is set out in the record dictionary.

## Hourly value

The valid readings of a site are grouped by the hour they count toward. An hour with at least
`hour_min_readings` valid readings is a valid hour, and its hourly value is the mean of the corrected
concentrations of those readings, rounded down to a whole number of tenths. Any other hour has no
hourly value.

## Local standard time

Every hour and day in this standard is an hour or day of local standard time, as the zone rules
define it, all year round. Daylight saving never moves an hour or a day of a report; civil time is
used only where a file says its times are written in it.

## Packets received

A unit has received a journal entry when one of the rows on its card was sent as that packet: the
entry's channel is the unit's channel, its minute is the true end of that row's averaging interval,
and its frame is the row's frame.

## Reconciliation residual

The reconciliation residual is the network's valid readings less its rolled-up valid readings.

## Report window

A reading lies inside the report window when the hour it counts toward lies in one of the days of
the report window.

## Required valid days

The required valid days are `days` times `site_min_valid_day_percent`, divided by 100 and rounded
up to a whole number.

## Site of a reading

A reading belongs to the site where its unit was deployed at the instant the reading represents. A
reading at an instant when its unit stood at no site belongs to no site.

## Site status

A site is in `nonattainment` when its design value is greater than the daily standard. Otherwise it
is in `attainment` when its valid days are at least the required valid days. Otherwise it is
`incomplete`.

## Valid reading

A reading is valid when it lies inside the report window, belongs to a site, has a `pm25_raw` value,
has an empty `status`, has a span factor in force for its unit at the instant it represents, and
that instant lies inside no maintenance window of its site.

## Worst site

The worst site of a group of sites is the site with the highest design value among those that have
one; of two sites with the same design value, the one whose site id carries the lower number is the
worse. A group in which no site has a design value has no worst site.

# Record dictionary

This dictionary describes every file of an extract of the Harbourline PM2.5 network, field by field.
The monitoring standard (`monitoring_standard.md`) says how the records become daily values and
compliance findings, and the report layout (`report_layout.md`) says what the reports hold.

## Conventions shared by every file

- Text files are UTF-8 with LF line endings. A file may begin with a byte-order mark, which belongs
  to no field.
- CSV files are comma separated, quote fields in the usual CSV way, and carry one header row.
- An empty CSV field means the value is absent.
- Times are written `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM` and name whole minutes. A time is a true UTC time unless its
  column or file says otherwise.
- Where a row covers a period between two times, the period includes its first time and excludes
  its last time, unless this dictionary says otherwise for that file.
- Field values are compared exactly as written, except channel labels (see Unit deployments).
- Rows of a CSV file are in no particular order.
- Site ids are the letter `S` followed by a whole number written without leading zeros. Unit ids are
  `AQ` followed by digits. Asset tags are `T-` followed by digits. Entry ids are `E` followed by a
  whole number. No unit id is also an asset tag.

## Asset tags (`asset_tags.csv`)

Columns: `asset_tag`, `unit_id`, `valid_from_utc`, `valid_to_utc`.

Each row says that the asset tag names the unit from `valid_from_utc` up to `valid_to_utc`; an empty
`valid_to_utc` means the row has no end. A tag names at most one unit at any instant, and a tag may
name different units at different times.

## Card readings (`card_readings.csv`)

Columns: `unit_id`, `card_stamp`, `frame`, `pm25_raw`, `status`.

One row is one reading taken from the memory card of a sensor unit. A unit averages the
concentration over consecutive 15-minute intervals and writes one row at the end of each interval.
A card holds no two rows with the same stamp. A unit that is powered off writes nothing, so a card
can have gaps.

- `card_stamp` is the reading of the unit's own clock at the end of the reading's averaging
  interval. A unit's clock is set to UTC but runs a whole number of minutes ahead of or behind true
  UTC, never by more than 19 minutes either way. The clock reading minus the true UTC time is the
  unit's clock offset, and the offset holds for every row on the unit's card. The true end of a
  reading's averaging interval is its card stamp minus the clock offset.
- The instant a reading represents is the true end of its averaging interval. When that instant
  falls exactly on the hour, the reading represents the hour that the interval closes, not the hour
  that begins at that instant.
- `frame` is the unit's packet counter for the reading, a whole number from 0 to 255. It goes up by
  one for each row the unit writes and returns to 0 after 255.
- `pm25_raw` is the uncorrected concentration in tenths of a microgram per cubic metre, a
  non-negative whole number. It is empty when the sensor produced no value for the interval.
- `status` is empty for an ordinary reading. Any other value is a letter code that marks the
  interval as a zero check, a span check or a sensor fault.

## Extract manifest (`extract_manifest.json`)

A JSON object with the keys `network` (text), `report_id` (text), `first_day` (a date written
`YYYY-MM-DD`), `days` (a whole number), `span_register_revision` (text) and `policy_revision`
(text). The report window is the run of `days` consecutive days of local standard time that starts
with `first_day`. The two revisions name the span register and the network policy this extract was
cut against.

## Maintenance log (`maintenance_log.json`)

A JSON object with the keys `time_basis` and `entries`.

- `time_basis` is `civil` or `utc` and gives the clock every time in the file is written in: civil
  time of the network zone, or true UTC.
- `entries` is a list of objects with the keys `site_id`, `begins`, `ends` and `work`. Each entry is a
  maintenance window at the site, from `begins` up to `ends`. `work` is free text.
- No `begins` or `ends` time falls in an hour that civil time skips or repeats.

## Network policy (`network_policy*.json`)

One or more JSON files whose names begin with `network_policy`. Each holds one object with the keys
`revision` (text), `daily_standard` (a concentration in micrograms per cubic metre, written as decimal
text with one digit after the point), `hour_min_readings`, `day_min_hours` and
`site_min_valid_day_percent` (whole numbers). The policy in force is the file whose `revision` is
the revision the manifest names.

## Receipt journal (`receipt_journal.csv`)

Columns: `channel`, `received_utc`, `frame`.

The network base station writes one row for each radio packet it receives. A unit sends one packet
for each row it writes on its card, on the unit's channel, in the true minute at which that row's
averaging interval ends, and the packet carries the row's frame. The station records a received
packet once, on the channel it arrived on, at the true minute it was sent, with the packet's frame.
Packets can be lost, and the station never records a packet that no unit sent. No channel ever
carries two packets in the same minute.

## Site register (`site_register.csv`)

Columns: `site_id`, `site_name`, `district`, `operator_class`.

Every monitoring site of the network. `district` is the district the site lies in; the reporting
districts are `North`, `South`, `West` and `East`, and a site in any other district belongs to no
district rollup. `operator_class` is `commercial` for a fence-line monitor run under a facility permit
and `public` for an agency monitor.

## Span registers (`span_register*.csv`)

One or more CSV files whose names begin with `span_register`, with the columns `revision`,
`entry_id`, `unit_ref`, `effective_utc`, `span_factor`, `action` and `target_entry`. Every row of one
file carries that file's revision, and the span register in force is the file whose revision is the
revision the manifest names. The register's entries, read as a whole, give each unit's span factor
over time.

- `action` is `set`, `amend` or `withdraw`. Columns an action does not use are empty.
- A `set` entry gives the span factor `span_factor` (decimal text) to the unit `unit_ref` from the
  instant `effective_utc` on. `unit_ref` is a unit id or an asset tag. An asset tag stands for the unit
  the tag names at `effective_utc`; an entry whose tag names no unit at that instant does nothing.
- An `amend` entry replaces the span factor of the `set` entry that `target_entry` names with its own
  `span_factor`; the unit and the effective instant stay those of the set entry. When several live
  amend entries name one set entry, the one with the highest entry number governs. An amend entry
  that names anything other than a set entry does nothing.
- A `withdraw` entry makes the `set` or `amend` entry that `target_entry` names void: a void entry
  counts as if it were absent. A withdraw entry that names a withdraw entry, or names no entry of the
  register, does nothing.
- A unit's span factor in force at an instant comes from the live set entry for that unit with the
  latest effective instant at or before that instant; an entry takes effect at its effective instant.
  Of two live set entries for one unit with the same effective instant, the higher entry number
  governs. A unit with no live set entry at or before an instant has no span factor in force then.

## Unit deployments (`unit_deployments.csv`)

Columns: `unit_id`, `site_id`, `channel`, `installed_utc`, `removed_utc`.

Each row says that the unit stood at the site from `installed_utc` up to `removed_utc`; an empty
`removed_utc` means the unit is still in place. A unit's deployments never overlap, and every unit
with a card appears here. A unit keeps one radio channel throughout. Channel labels are compared
without regard to letter case and to spaces before or after the label; a label's written form is the
label in capital letters with those spaces removed.

## Zone rules (`zone_rules.json`)

A JSON object with the keys `standard_offset_minutes` and `daylight_saving`.

- Local standard time is true UTC plus `standard_offset_minutes`.
- `daylight_saving` is a list of objects with the keys `begins`, `ends` and `shift_minutes`.
  Daylight saving is in force from `begins` up to `ends`, both written in civil time. Civil time is local standard time plus `shift_minutes` while
  daylight saving is in force, and local standard time at all other times.

# Report layout

A run over one extract writes nine reports, in this order: `summary.json`, `policy_audit.json`,
`data_quality.json`, `region_North.json`, `region_South.json`, `region_West.json`,
`region_East.json`, `segment_commercial.json` and `segment_public.json`. The four region reports are
the district rollups and the two segment reports are the operator rollups of the monitoring
standard. Every report is written on every run.

## Bytes of a report

Each report is one JSON object written in UTF-8, in the form Python's `json` module produces with
sorted keys and an indent of two spaces, followed by one line feed:

- every object member and list item on its own line, indented two spaces per level of nesting;
- object keys in sorted order, each followed by a colon and one space;
- a comma at the end of every member or item line except the last of its object or list;
- an empty object written `{}` and an empty list written `[]`;
- text written with double quotes, every character outside printable ASCII written as a `\u`
  escape;
- whole numbers written in decimal with no leading zeros, `true`, `false` and `null` written as
  such, and no other number forms.

Lists are in the order this document gives. A key marked "or null" holds `null` when the value it
names does not exist.

## Standard output

While it writes the reports, the program prints one line per report to standard output, in the
order above: the report's file name, one space, and the number of bytes written to that report.
It prints nothing else to standard output.

## `summary.json`

- `report_id`, `first_day`: text, copied from the extract manifest.
- `days`: whole number, copied from the extract manifest.
- `card_rows`: whole number, the rows of the card readings file.
- `valid_readings`: whole number, the valid readings.
- `valid_hours`: whole number, the valid hours over all sites.
- `valid_days`: whole number, the valid days over all sites.
- `exceedance_days`: whole number, the exceedance days over all sites.
- `site_status_counts`: object with the keys `attainment`, `incomplete` and `nonattainment`, each the
  number of sites with that site status.
- `highest_design_value`: object with the keys `site_id` (text or null) and `value` (whole number or
  null): the worst site of the whole network and its design value.
- `reconciliation`: object with the keys `card_rows`, `excluded_readings`, `valid_readings`,
  `rolled_up_valid_readings` and `residual`, all whole numbers: the card rows, the excluded
  readings, the valid readings, the rolled-up valid readings and the reconciliation residual.

## `policy_audit.json`

- `policy_revision`: text, the revision of the policy in force.
- `span_register_revision`: text, the revision of the span register in force.
- `daily_standard_tenths`: whole number, the daily standard.
- `hour_min_readings`, `day_min_hours`: whole numbers, copied from the policy in force.
- `required_valid_days`: whole number, the required valid days.
- `sites`: list with one object per site of the site register, ordered by the number in the site id,
  with the keys `site_id`, `district`, `operator_class` (text, copied from the site register),
  `valid_days`, `complete_days`, `exceedance_days` (whole numbers), `design_rank` (whole number or
  null), `design_value` (whole number or null) and `status` (text, the site status).

## `data_quality.json`

- `journal_entries`: whole number, the rows of the receipt journal.
- `excluded_readings`: whole number, the excluded readings.
- `exclusions`: object with the keys `outside_window`, `undeployed`, `missing_value`, `flagged`,
  `uncalibrated` and `maintenance`, each the number of rows counted under that reason.
- `units`: list with one object per unit that has a card, ordered by unit id compared as text, with
  the keys `unit_id` (text), `channel` (text, the written form of the unit's channel label),
  `card_rows` (whole number, the rows on the unit's card) and `received_packets` (whole number, the journal entries the unit has
  received).

## Region and segment reports

A region report carries the key `district` and a segment report the key `operator_class`, holding
the district or operator class the report covers. An out-of-scope report holds that key and
`in_scope` with the value `false`, and nothing else. An in-scope report holds that key and:

- `in_scope`: `true`.
- `sites`: list with one object per site of the rollup, ordered by the number in the site id, with
  the keys `site_id` (text), `status` (text, the site status), `valid_readings` (whole number, the
  valid readings belonging to the site) and `daily_values` (object whose keys are the site's valid
  days written `YYYY-MM-DD` and whose values are the daily values).
- `valid_readings`: whole number, the valid readings of the rollup's sites.
- `exceedance_days`: whole number, the exceedance days of the rollup's sites.
- `worst_site`: object with the keys `site_id` (text) and `design_value` (whole number) for the worst
  site of the rollup, or null.
- `status`: text, the rollup status.
