## Setting
A city bus operator plans overnight and midday charging for its electric fleet across four depots (North, South, West, East) after a disruption notice (a charger out of service, a feeder derating on given slots, extra service trips). The day is split into stated 15-minute slots. Each bus has an arrival slot, a departure slot, energy needed by departure (whole kWh units), a maximum charge per slot by bus type, and a class (commercial route or public contract: school, hospital shuttle; stated table). Each depot has a stated number of chargers (a charger serves one bus per slot). The tariff per kWh is stated per slot. All four depots hang off ONE grid feeder with a stated limit in kWh per slot (the coupling).

Objective, stated exactly and lexicographically so the optimum is unique: (1) minimise total unmet energy at departure weighted by the stated class weight (a bus that leaves short is listed with a stated reason string), (2) then minimise total energy cost, (3) then minimise the number of buses whose plan changed from the published plan, (4) then the stated tie-break (lexicographically smallest list of (slot, bus id, kWh)).
