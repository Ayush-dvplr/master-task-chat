- Classify a judged criterion by what it inspects before reading its `type` label: R5's "task completion" label made the first gate 01 draft wrongly call it an unstated outcome rule (fixed after reading 04_Judge_QC.md:99).
- Probe what the instruction promises but no test grades (plain decode on damaged frames); that surfaced DS-20-1, which no script or check sees.
- A CL-4 one-fact-wrong probe in the warm verifier image (71/107) costs about a minute and gives the number CL-4 asks for.
- Separate-mode verifiers see only declared artifacts, so any negative that scans the workspace for extra files is dead weight (TS-16-1, still open since v4).
