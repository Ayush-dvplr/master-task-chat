# threshold reconciliation converges when the step change is within epsilon
convergence_epsilon = 0
max_iterations = 64
iteration_rule = t_next = base_threshold + floor((contribution + t) / iteration_divisor)
seed_value = base_threshold
base_threshold = base_threshold of the threshold_tier_curve.parquet row whose tier equals the role_level of the resolved principal
iteration_divisor = fixed by the curve reports in override_deltas.json: it is the divisor under which every standing curve report, its curve computed for the role it names (that role's param_key, tenant and role_level in principal_role_index.yaml) by the rules that give the resolved principal its curve, converges to its reported converged value; with no standing curve report it is iteration_divisor in reconciliation_rules.conf. When the reports fix no single divisor, the threshold is unresolved: iterations holds only the seed value, and converged and risk_threshold are null
