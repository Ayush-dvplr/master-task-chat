# Errata, issued 2031-07-21

These are corrections to the rule documents of this corpus. They describe the
contract as it has stood over the whole horizon, so they apply to every cycle
and not only to cycles after the date of issue. Where a rule document and this
errata differ, this errata governs.

## 1. Replaces section 2 of rounding_rules.md

Section 2 of `rounding_rules.md` describes the conversion as applied one step
at a time with a rounding at each step. That description was wrong and is
replaced by the following.

The conversion of a stated figure to wafer equivalents happens once for the
whole walk. Multiply the stated figure by the product of the `factor_per_100`
values of every step the walk took, divide by one hundred raised to the number
of steps, and round half up. A figure converts once, from its stated value,
however many steps the walk took. No intermediate figure is kept and no
intermediate figure is rounded.

## 2. Adds to section 3 of commitment_rules.md

Section 3 of `commitment_rules.md` gives the honouring order but does not say
what happens to an agreement with no rank in force in a cycle. Such an
agreement is honoured after every ranked agreement, taking the rank declared
as `unranked_commitment_rank` in `derivation_policy.ini`, with ties among
those agreements broken by ascending agreement id as usual.

## 3. Adds to section 1 of ceiling_rules.md

Section 1 of `ceiling_rules.md` names `declaring_site` as the site whose clock
reads the wall stamp of a ceiling row. That is its only role. A ceiling is a
property of a customer and an epoch and governs the requests of that customer
and epoch at every site, whatever site declared it.
