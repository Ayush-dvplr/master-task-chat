# Practice note, withdrawn

This note described how the apportionment was run before the contract in this
corpus was written. It is kept so that older figures can be read. It is not
part of the contract and nothing in it governs any cycle of this horizon.
Where it disagrees with a rule document or with `errata_2031-07.md`, it is the
note that is wrong.

## How the old desk worked

Capacity was shared out in proportion to the quantities asked for. Each
request received the share of capacity that its quantity bore to the total
quantity asked at that site in that period, cut down to a whole number of
wafers and never above what the request claimed, and the remainder went to
the largest request by quantity that could still use it, ties broken by the
ascending reported reference. Committed minimums were paid out of that share
rather than before it, so a small customer with a minimum often received less
than the minimum.

Quantities were taken from whichever system had most recently touched the
record, and the desk kept no record of which system that was. A node that a
site could not run was substituted one step at a time, with the figure
rounded back to whole wafers after each step, which is why some older
equivalent figures are one or two wafers higher than the figures this corpus
produces.

Entitlement ceilings were read as a ceiling of the site that declared them,
so a customer with two sites was treated as having one ceiling at each.

Unmet demand was not carried. A period closed and whatever was not granted was
dropped, which is why the old reports carry no deficit column and no running
totals.

The old desk also read the second quantity column of the order book, which was
the only column its intake tool could see.
