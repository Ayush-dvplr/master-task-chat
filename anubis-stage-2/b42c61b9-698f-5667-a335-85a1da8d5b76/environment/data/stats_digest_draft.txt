DRAFT - readout statistics digest (prepared 2026-09-01, not adopted)

Decision parameters: alpha 0.10 two-sided, Bonferroni within each experiment, SRM alpha 0.01.
Allocation is 50/50 for every two-arm test and equal thirds for three-arm tests.
All metrics use a 14 day attribution window from the first in-window exposure.
Winsorize per arm at the interpolated 99th percentile (statistics.quantiles, method exclusive).
CUPED theta is fitted separately in each arm.
Revenue is converted at the flat snapshot in fx_rates_snapshot.json.
Units flagged in bot_watchlist are excluded; the legacy registry is authoritative for account types.
Relative lift is the difference over the treatment mean.
Identity links apply for the whole readout whatever their linked_on date; a link loop is broken at its first id.
A refund reversal subtracts its own amount from the order's refunds.
CHF is quoted per US dollar like JPY; FX uses the FX-R1 release until the final fixings land.
Launch slots follow the readout_owner of the readout calendar, ranked by lift.
CUPED theta is carried at full precision; rounding happens only when writing.
Sample ratio checks with three degrees of freedom use exp(-chi2/2).
