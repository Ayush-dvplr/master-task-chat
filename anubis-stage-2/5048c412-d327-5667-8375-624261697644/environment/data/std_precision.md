# Precision standard

Storages, transfers, residuals, head offsets, damping percentages and loss rates are integers. Every intermediate quotient (a mean of storages, a draw share, a damped residual, a loss) is exact until the rounding its standard names; no value is rounded at any other point, and no binary floating-point value enters any figure.

Two roundings are named in the documents. Rounding a value to whole megalitres or to hundredths follows the glossary entry for rounded values. Loss rounding drops the fraction of a kilolitre: a loss of 431.97 kL becomes 431 kL.
