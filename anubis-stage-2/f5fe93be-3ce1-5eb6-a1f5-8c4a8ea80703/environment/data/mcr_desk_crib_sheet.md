# MCR desk crib sheet (informal, not authoritative)

These are the quick notes the master control room kept taped to the desk during the old close cycle. They are shorthand, they were never reconciled against the current rule documents, and several of them are known to be loose. They are kept here only because operators still refer to them out of habit. Nothing on this page governs this close; where it disagrees with the rule documents in `corpus_index.md`, those documents govern.

Desk shorthand:

- Air check: if chain A logged the spot `PLAYED`, treat it as aired; only look at chain B when chain A has nothing for the spot.
- Dating: file a spot under the calendar date printed on its as-run label.
- Windows: a spot is in daypart when its start label falls inside the daypart bounds.
- Chain B: read chain B labels straight off the log; the probe sheet is for engineering, not for traffic.
- Drop frame: for a quick count treat every label as `((HH*60+MM)*60+SS)*30+FF`; the skipped-label detail only matters at month end.
- Corrections: when two corrections clash, keep the one that came in last.
- Make-goods: push a make-good into whichever break in the daypart has the most room left.
- Billing: net each order line's bonus spots against its short days across the week before raising make-goods, and take commission once on the advertiser's invoice total.

Operators who need the exact treatment should ignore this sheet and work from the rule documents.
