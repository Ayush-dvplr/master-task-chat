# Legacy runbook

This runbook describes how the desk settled campaigns before the current contract. It is kept
for reference only and governs nothing in this corpus.

The old desk took the latest requisition row for a block and round whatever its recording time,
read the species and age class from the requisition itself, and applied the handling ceiling to
the head count before the dose factor. Scarce doses were shared pro rata: each claim received
its claimable quantity multiplied by the available doses and divided by the total claimable
quantity, truncated to a whole dose, with any remainder granted to the largest claim. Carried
quantities were held for three rounds and then dropped. Lots were drawn newest first so that
the freshest stock reached the field, and a lot whose serotype differed from the campaign target
was treated as unusable.

None of the above decides anything today. The current contract and the rule documents it names
decide everything.
