# PD-RETAIL v4 performance monitoring - cycle 2026-Q2 specification

Status: in force. Owner: Retail Credit Risk Model Monitoring. This specification is the authority for every artifact of the cycle. Policies referenced by name are part of the specification. Where a policy states a precedence rule, that rule decides conflicts between sources.

## 1. Deliverables

All CSV deliverables are UTF-8, comma separated, LF line endings, a header row, no quoting (no value contains a comma), a trailing newline, and rows sorted as stated. Every JSON deliverable is UTF-8, indented with two spaces, keys sorted, with a trailing newline. Decimal strings use a point and exactly the stated number of decimals. Empty optional CSV values are empty strings.

Field rules. acct is the canonical account key (3.1). segment is the product at decision (3.4). cohort_month is YYYY-MM of the local decision date. decision_utc and score_ts_utc are YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SSZ. score and score_band are integers, bad is 0 or 1, weight is an integer. exposure_eur has two decimals. window_start and window_end are YYYY-MM, bad_month is YYYY-MM or empty. Integers in JSON are JSON numbers; bad_rate, exposure_bad_rate, auc, gini, ks, psi, expected and actual are strings with six decimals; exposure_eur in JSON is a string with two decimals; ks_score is an integer. population_summary.json and discrimination.json carry the six segments and ALL; psi.json and calibration.csv carry the six segments only. calibration.csv leaves actual_rate and expected_rate empty for a band without accounts. status is RECONCILED or UNRECONCILED.

Population of each deliverable. exclusions.csv lists every excluded account with its first reason in the order of exclusion policy section 1. score_selection.csv lists every account that reached score selection (not excluded as WINDOW, WITHDRAWN or DUPLICATE). performance_windows.csv lists every account that reached outcome classification (bad definition standard section 4), with the derived outcome BAD, GOOD, CENSORED, INSUFFICIENT or INDETERMINATE before any audit override. cohort.csv lists every account that is not excluded; its bad column carries the final label after audit overrides.

Publication gate. When the delivery controls (section 2) do not reconcile, no metric may be published: cohort.csv, exclusions.csv, score_selection.csv, performance_windows.csv and calibration.csv contain only their header rows, the three metric JSON files contain {"segments": {}, "status": "UNRECONCILED"}, and reconciliation.json is still written in full.

## 2. Delivery controls

2.1 Every delivery of the cycle carries a control block naming its own control_id and acknowledging two other deliveries: ack_rows (the record count of the delivery named by ack_rows_of) and ack_hash (the hash total of the delivery named by ack_hash_of). The block sits where the catalogue (section 9) says: a '# CONTROL' comment line, a _control JSON line, a control element, a delivery_control table or section, the delivery_control view of a SQLite file, the delivery_control key-value metadata of a Parquet file, the custom document properties of a workbook or Word file, the last text line of a PDF, the delivery-control meta element of an HTML file, the Delivery control section of a Markdown file, the type 99 trailer of a fixed-width file, the DELIVERY CONTROL message of the mailbox, or the X-DELIVERY-CONTROL property of the calendar.

2.2 The record count of a delivery is the number of its records as defined in the catalogue: every record counts, including duplicates, superseded, voided, quarantined and out-of-scope ones. Headers, re-emitted headers, comment lines, blank lines, stack-trace continuation lines, heartbeat lines, control blocks, INI DEFAULT keys and repeated page or table headers are not records. A quoted field may contain line breaks and still belongs to one record. The hash total is the sum over all records of the integer formed by the decimal digits of the record's hash key, read in order (AC-0012345 gives 12345, 2025-03-31 gives 20250331). Keys are read after decoding the file in its documented encoding.

2.3 The deliveries of the cycle are the deliveries of cycle 2026-Q2-MON in delivery_manifest.json; older cycles in the manifest are history. The controls reconcile when every delivery of the cycle is present and readable, and for every control_id of the cycle the acknowledged record count and hash total both equal the values computed from that delivery. failed_controls lists every control_id for which this does not hold. files_checked is the number of deliveries read; estate_record_total and estate_hash_total are the sums of the computed record counts and hash totals over the deliveries read.

## 3. Accounts and the cohort

3.1 Keys. An account key is canonical as AC followed by seven digits. Before comparing, remove all whitespace including no-break spaces and zero-width spaces, upper-case, map the Cyrillic letters U+0410 and U+0421 to A and C, remove hyphens, and write the digits as an integer left-padded to seven digits (ac-00123456, AC 0123456 and АС0123456 are all AC0123456). Customer keys (CU + seven digits) follow the same rule. Product codes, statuses and dispositions are case-insensitive and are compared after trimming.

3.2 Applications. The universe is every account that appears in the six application partitions applications_2025_01 to applications_2025_06. Records quarantined in the data quality issue log are ignored. When an account appears more than once, the record with the highest revision wins; between equal revisions the later received instant wins; between equal receipts the record from the later delivery (delivered_at in the manifest) wins, and within one delivery the later record.

3.3 Cohort window and time. The market of an account is the market of its branch in the 2025 branch directory, using the directory row whose validity (from and to, both inclusive, empty to open) contains the decision date; for this lookup use the UTC date of the decision when the partition records UTC instants and the recorded local date when it records local time. Branch codes of the 2026 scheme are first mapped to their 2025 code. Markets keep wall-clock time as follows: GB is UTC+0 and every other market UTC+1; all markets add one hour from 01:00 UTC on the last Sunday of March to 01:00 UTC on the last Sunday of October. A local time inside the spring gap is read with the standard offset. The cohort is every decision whose local date falls in [2025-01-01, 2025-07-01); others are excluded as WINDOW. cohort_month is the month of the local decision date.

3.4 Product. A product code maps to its segment through product_code_crosswalk.ini. A partition whose catalogue entry says product basis: current records the code at extraction; the code at decision is recovered from product_migrations_2025.xlsx as the from_code of the earliest migration of that account effective after the decision date (ties by migration id); with no such migration the recorded code stands.

3.5 Withdrawals. An account with a withdrawal dated 0 to 14 days after its decision date is excluded as WITHDRAWN unless the withdrawal was reversed. Later withdrawals are closures handled by the servicing feeds and are ignored here.

3.6 Duplicates. The customer of an account is resolved through the customer master alias table and then the approved merge decisions (customer merge decisions document). Among accounts that survive 3.3 and 3.5, group by resolved customer and segment and walk each group in order of decision instant (ties by acct): an account whose decision date is at most 30 days after the decision date of the last kept account of its group is excluded as DUPLICATE; otherwise it is kept. Only kept accounts restart the 30-day window.

## 4. File conventions

4.1 Text encodings are UTF-8 unless the catalogue says ISO-8859-1; a UTF-8 byte order mark may precede the first line. Line endings may be LF or CRLF, also within one file.

4.2 Delimited files: lines starting with '#' are comments or controls; the first other line is the header. Quoting follows RFC 4180. A later line whose fields are exactly the header's column names in any order is a re-emitted header and defines the column order of the records that follow it. Trailing empty fields beyond the header width are ignored. Thousands separators are commas inside quoted values; negative values may be written in parentheses.

4.3 Not-reported DPD is written -1, blank, N/A, null, "null" or 9999 depending on the catalogue; 999 is the written-off sentinel (bad definition standard 2.3).

4.4 YAML registries are read with every scalar as a string (YAML 1.1 readers turn NO into false and some numbers into other types). INI files are read without interpolation and with case-insensitive keys. XML namespaces are as declared.

4.5 Identifiers other than account and customer keys (event, run, application, branch, grant, case, employee, amendment, migration, hold, change request and delivery ids) are compared with all whitespace removed and in upper case, and are reported in that form.

4.6 Account money amounts (limits, new limits, balances and charge-off amounts) are read to cents: a value written with a third decimal is rounded half up to cents when it is read. Fixing values and other rates are not money and are read exactly.

## 5. Performance snapshots

5.1 For each month and market exactly one delivery is certified (certified_snapshot_partitions in the manifest). Only certified records supply snapshot values; a record for an account outside the certified market scope of its delivery is not a snapshot of that account.

5.2 Within one certified delivery the later record for the same account and period wins. Corrections, restatements and replicas are governed by the governance precedence policy.

## 6. Scores

6.1 The score of an account is chosen from the scoring events of every engine node. The run register (model_registry_v4.toml and scoring_runs_2025q2.toml) is the system of record for each run's node, model version and status; an event whose run is not registered is invalid, and a model field carried by an event is informational only.

6.2 An event is invalid when its run status is not OK, when its run was voided, when its run belongs to node 9, or when it is quarantined.

6.3 The model version of the run must be approved for the account's segment on the local decision date under the approvals of the in-force registry and not withdrawn on that date.

6.4 Point in time: the corrected score instant (recorded instant plus the run's clock correction) must not be later than the decision instant. Among valid events the latest corrected instant wins; ties go to the larger numeric part of event_id. An account without a valid event is excluded as NO_SCORE. score_ts_utc reports the corrected instant.

## 7. Weights and exposure

Design weights follow the sampling methodology; exposure follows the limit management policy. Weighted counts use the design weight of each account.

## 8. Metrics

8.1 Score bands. Each segment has nine cut points c1 < ... < c9 (model documentation, as amended by approved errata). Band 1 is score <= c1, band k is c(k-1) < score <= ck, band 10 is score > c9.

8.2 Population. accounts and bads count cohort accounts; weighted_accounts and weighted_bads sum design weights; bad_rate = weighted_bads / weighted_accounts. exposure_eur is the sum of weight times exposure; exposure_bad_rate is the weighted exposure of bad accounts over weighted exposure.

8.3 Discrimination. A higher score means lower risk. auc is the weighted probability that a good account scores higher than a bad account, counting ties as one half: sum over bad i and good j of w_i w_j ([s_j > s_i] + 0.5 [s_j = s_i]) divided by (sum of bad weights times sum of good weights). gini = 2 auc - 1, computed from the unrounded auc. ks is the maximum over distinct scores t of |F_bad(t) - F_good(t)| with weighted cumulative shares F(t) of accounts scoring at most t; ks_score is the smallest t that attains it.

8.4 Stability. For each segment, expected shares come from the development distribution (Appendix A) aggregated into the score bands; actual shares are the weighted shares of cohort accounts per band. Before the logarithm both shares are floored at 0.0001; psi = sum over bands of (a - e) ln(a / e). The bins report the unfloored shares.

8.5 Calibration. Each account's expected PD is the calibration grid value for its segment and score in the model documentation, replaced where calibration Appendix B of the 2026 reissue publishes a retrospective correction; where Appendix B lists the same segment and score more than once, the row listed last stands. expected_bads = sum of weight times PD per band, actual_rate = weighted_bads / weighted_accounts and expected_rate = expected_bads / weighted_accounts.

8.6 Rounding. Compute with exact rational or high-precision decimal arithmetic (at least 28 significant digits) and round only the reported value, half to even, to six decimals; money is rounded half up to cents at the account level and summed exactly.
