factor_micro and quantum-yield are integers stored x1e6; divide by 1e6 before use. drift_ppm is parts-per-million (divide by 1e6) and is subtracted. calibrated_flux_ujy is integer micro-jansky. full_well_counts is a raw-count ceiling in counts, used as published and never divided. weight_micro is an exposure weight stored x1e6 whose scale cancels in the attribution ratio, so it too is used as published.
