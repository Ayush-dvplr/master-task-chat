## Load-bearing work

The stream must be reproduced exactly. One xorshift generator is constructed per step from a mixing hash of the run seed and the step index, and four blocks are drawn in a fixed order, the third of which consumes nothing at all when the bias is disabled. Missing that conditional shifts every jitter draw and every number downstream of it.

Routing must be reproduced exactly. The combine weights shift against the smallest quantised value over every expert for the token, not over the selected ones, and are rounded independently rather than renormalised.

The two sealed decisions must be recovered by enumeration. Twenty four permutations of the tie break fields and one hundred eighty cause orders permitted by the three recorded partial order constraints are the search spaces. Each candidate is scored by recomputing the record the digest covers and serialising it canonically. The digests validate the whole upstream pipeline rather than shortcutting it, because a wrong router or a wrong sweep reproduces nothing.

The settlement must come out of the sweep. ## Root cause

The deployed orchestrator follows the superseded capacity revision, so it holds at the minimum before aligning and never produces an unaligned capacity. It follows the superseded guard revision, so promotion always lags by one shape. It places experts on ranks by integer division of the expert index rather than by walking the group sorted list against the affinity order. It sources the balance term from accepted assignments rather than from demand. On top of those four it runs with a capacity factor of two, a bucket of three, a minimum capacity of ten, a cache limit of eight and a promotion threshold of twenty four, and the last two of those together guarantee that the live guard key set never fits in the cache and that promotion never fires. The consequence is a permanent eviction and recompilation cycle: three hundred nineteen recompilations across the run, two hundred sixty four of them after the warmup bound, one hundred ninety five evictions, the budget first exceeded at step six. Every recompiled step carries a graph break, so every capture attempt fails with the stream capture cause and the replay discount is never earned. Cached entries and their guard closures accumulate without bound and the host headroom goes to minus one hundred seventy five megabytes and stays negative for ninety nine of the hundred steps. The published summary came out of the orchestrator's own walk, so every helper above shaped it as well: it reports 361 recompilations, 291 of them after warmup, a smallest headroom of minus 96468992 bytes, a throughput ratio of 0.092069 and a closing loss of 1.505604368718, where the correct replay of the same configuration gives 319, 264, minus 175898624, 0.101717 and 1.505659250459. Only the drop rate agrees, at zero.

The settlement finds eight admissible candidates and selects a capacity factor of one and a quarter, a bucket of eight, both markable dimensions dynamic and the reduce overhead mode, which clears all four gates at a throughput ratio of 2.31673 with a closing loss of 1.505659250459 and zero recompilations after warmup.

## Pitfalls

Holding at the minimum before aligning. Aligning the minimum itself. Using one capacity reduction for both the guard key and the collective payload. Dividing the expert index to place experts. Counting distinct shapes without the pending one. Believing a promoted dimension never specialises again. Sourcing the balance term from accepted assignments. Treating an empty expert as a zero in the dispersion. Dividing the expert accumulator by the capacity rather than by the accepted count. Saturating the eight bit payload before rounding it. Rounding once at serialisation instead of at every named step. Clamping the signed headroom or the signed margins at zero. Reading the warmup window and the gate window with the same inclusivity. Restarting the pool peak before the figure for the step is published. Comparing the sweep's probe horizon parity against the convergence band, or the settled gate verdict against the settlement band or over the probe horizon. Running the final selection key in the same direction as the four before it. Treating `expert_shape_matrix` as a matrix. Treating the digests as an answer key rather than as a check on the pipeline. Producing the records from a throwaway script rather than from the delivered module.
