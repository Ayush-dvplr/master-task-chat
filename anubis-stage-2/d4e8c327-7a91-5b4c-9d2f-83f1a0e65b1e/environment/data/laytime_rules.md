# Laytime rules

Laytime hours are the duration the vessel is on hire. The governing laytime of a Notice of Readiness is the first laytime_hours column of the NOR log, by position. A later restatement in the registry may be present and governs only where the NOR log is silent. The weather-stoppage convention before the April cutover is not stated in this document; the settlement log laytime_observations.log settles it by enumeration. The candidates are SHEX (Sunday hours do not count), SHINC (every hour counts) and WWD (hours of adverse weather do not count).
