# Data dictionary - Sentinel training-set sources

## Identifier canonicalization

Every identifier is canonicalized before use: remove leading and trailing ASCII whitespace and no-break spaces (U+00A0, U+202F), then upper-case. Accounts are 'AC-' followed by the number left-padded with zeros to 6 digits; merchants 'MR-' plus 5 digits; legacy core-banking accounts 'L-' plus 7 digits; devices 'DV-' plus 8 hex digits; IP references 'IP-' plus 7 hex digits; currencies are 3-letter codes. An identifier that still contains any non-ASCII character after this, or that does not match its pattern, is invalid: the record is excluded with reason invalid_key. Merged-away accounts resolve through the merge chain (account merge log MERGE entries and non-identity rows of the merge map, followed until a live account is reached); merged-away merchants resolve through merged_into chains (merchant master, and the merchant snapshot the governance charter grants merges). Legacy core-banking accounts resolve through the legacy_id crosswalk of the account registry snapshot the charter grants crosswalk authority. A resolved account must be listed by the account registries (base snapshot or revision) and a resolved merchant by the merchant master; otherwise the key is invalid. Every key of a reference table, register, log or document table (identifiers, record references, batch, task, case, decision, snapshot, partition, observation and erratum ids, status, entry, reason and MCC codes, zone names and aliases, currency codes, terminal ids) is matched case-insensitively after the same trimming.

## Sentinel values

ip_ref values '-', 'UNKNOWN', '0.0.0.0', 'null', 'n/a' or empty mean no IP; device values '', 'UNKNOWN' or null mean no device; entry code '????' means unknown entry mode (token UNKNOWN); a merchant risk cb_rate_bps of 'N/A' means the snapshot row carries no value and is skipped; an IP reputation score of -1 means not scored and the observation is skipped; a KYC tier of 9 means unknown and the review is skipped; a valid_to of '0000-00-00' means open-ended.

## Effective-dated tables

Effective-dated rows apply from valid_from (inclusive) to valid_to (exclusive). When several rows apply, the one with the latest valid_from wins.

## Status codes

Each event system reports its own raw status codes. The meaning of a code decides whether the record can become an example: approved and settled records are candidates; declined, reversed, refund and test map to the exclusion reasons declined, reversed, refund and test_transaction. Only the keeper's status counts.
