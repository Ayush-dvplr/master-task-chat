# Convergence

For each batch, settle the admitted analyte masses by damped proportional rebalance toward the fair share, halving the damping after two non-improving rounds, and reporting the round count. The damping seed is a governance constant.

The fair share splits a target mass across the analytes in proportion to what they already hold. The target is the batch mass when that mass sits at or below the batch cap, and the batch cap otherwise. Give each analyte its own mass times the target divided by the batch mass, keeping the floor of that division, then hand out the units still unassigned one each by largest remainder, ordering the analytes by the remainder of that same division from largest to smallest and breaking ties by analyte name ascending. A batch holding no mass has a fair share of zero for every analyte.

Settle in rounds, counting the rounds from zero and running at most sixty four of them. The residual of a round is the sum over analytes of the absolute difference between the mass an analyte holds and its fair share. A round counts as non-improving when its residual is greater than or equal to the previous residual, and the damping halves by floor division once two non-improving rounds have accumulated, after which that count starts again from zero. The halved damping already applies to the flow of the round in which the second non-improving round is counted. The flow for each analyte is its fair share minus the mass it holds, multiplied by the damping, divided by ten thousand and rounded half up away from zero, so the damping seed reads as basis points like the other scaled governance figures. When the flows across a batch do not sum to zero, subtract the difference from the analyte standing furthest from its fair share, measured by the absolute difference from largest to smallest and breaking ties by analyte name ascending, which keeps the batch mass whole.

Stop and report the current round index when the residual reaches zero, and report it the same way when a round yields a flow of zero for every analyte. When the round budget runs out with the batch still moving, report sixty four, the number of rounds run. A batch that cycles is reported unsettled with the lexicographically smallest state in the cycle and a round count of negative one, where the cycle begins at the first sighting of the repeated state and states compare as their masses read in analyte name order.
