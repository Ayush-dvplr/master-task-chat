A control plane operates an HTTP/3 reverse-proxy fleet that carries long-lived tenant streams across a sequence of scheduling epochs. Every epoch, every active stream must be assigned to exactly one node subject to node capacity, per-tenant fair-share, per-node token buckets, class-based preemption, migration penalties, and latency SLOs. Derive a discrete schedule that minimises the weighted sum of alpha times latency_cost, beta times migration_cost, and gamma times fairness_deviation under every hard constraint, then serialise a fixed set of report artifacts and chain them under an integrity manifest.

What you have. Under /app/data you will find the input corpus (fleet, streams, tenants, classes, latency, migration table, token buckets, epoch windows, constants, MTU matrix, rate profiles), a fully-traced 3-node 6-stream micro example in worked_micro_example.json, the complete written specification at /app/data/README-input-schema.md, and six sealed reference corpora (case-argosy.json through case-frigate.json) with companion seal files (seal-argosy.json through seal-frigate.json). Read the schema before writing any code.

Four rules that govern the algorithm's interior, one in the largest-remainder tie-break, one in the floor-versus-cap ordering, one in the SLO comparison, and one in the token-bucket refill rounding, were part of the original operational contract but their prose was lost from the shipped schema.
