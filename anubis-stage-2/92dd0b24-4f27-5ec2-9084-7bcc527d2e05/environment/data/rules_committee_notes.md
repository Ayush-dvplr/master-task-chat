# Rules Committee Errata

Each erratum amends a named section of `slate_contract.md` from its effective
instant. An erratum applies to the slate only when its recorded instant is at
or before the legality cutoff, on the same admission terms as any other
record. An erratum superseded by a later erratum against the same section no
longer applies.

## ER-0114

Section 5. Effective 2031-02-10T00:00:00Z. Recorded 2031-02-08T09:00:00Z.
The corrected instant of a receipt is obtained by subtracting the host offset
for the calendar date of the lane timestamp from that timestamp. The offset is
signed and is stated in seconds.

## ER-0119

Section 8. Effective 2031-02-14T00:00:00Z. Recorded 2031-02-12T11:30:00Z.
Superseded by ER-0137. A rider row applies when the authorising judge held
rank two or above at the row's recorded instant.

## ER-0123

Section 7. Effective 2031-02-21T00:00:00Z. Recorded 2031-02-19T16:45:00Z.
Set code normalisation applies to the registry side of the join as well as to
the entered side. A code that differs from a registry code only by case, by a
trailing asterisk or by leading zeros inside a trailing digit run names the
same set.

## ER-0128

Section 4. Effective 2031-03-03T00:00:00Z. Recorded 2031-03-01T08:15:00Z.
Retracted. Window ends were to be read as inclusive for the opening stage of a
season. This erratum was retracted before it took effect and the half open
reading stands.

## ER-0131

Section 13. Effective 2031-03-17T00:00:00Z. Recorded 2031-03-15T10:00:00Z.
The until date of a standing commitment is inclusive, so an occurrence falling
exactly on the until date is materialised on the same terms as any other
occurrence.

## ER-0137

Section 8. Effective 2031-04-07T00:00:00Z. Recorded 2031-04-04T13:20:00Z.
Supersedes ER-0119. A rider row applies when the authorising judge held rank
three or above at the row's recorded instant. Rank is read from the admitted
roster row with the latest effective instant at or before that recorded
instant.

## ER-0142

Section 11. Effective 2031-04-28T00:00:00Z. Recorded 2031-04-25T09:40:00Z.
A `lines` key present with the value null and a `lines` key absent are treated
alike and leave the list in force before that amendment unchanged. An empty
list clears the list.

## ER-0148

Section 14. Effective 2031-05-12T00:00:00Z. Recorded 2031-05-09T15:05:00Z.
Carry relief at a stage close is applied once per player per stage close and
the floor of the carry is zero. A player who held no submission effective
within the stage takes no relief at that close.

## ER-0153

Section 3. Effective 2031-08-01T00:00:00Z. Recorded 2031-07-28T07:00:00Z.
The competition day begins at 06:00 venue local.

## ER-0156

Section 10. Effective 2031-06-02T00:00:00Z. Recorded 2031-09-01T12:00:00Z.
Point charges are to be reported in whole points rather than centipoints.
