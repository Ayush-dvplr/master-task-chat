# KVEC November 2025 Settlement Data Contract

Kestrel Valley Electric Cooperative (KVEC) settles every month from the files in this directory. This contract defines the
computation and the deliverables. The settlement procedures manual (`kvec_settlement_procedures_manual.docx`, revision 3)
defines which source is authoritative when sources disagree; each head-end profile defines how its export is parsed. Read
all three. Nothing in a file name, file date or file size decides authority.

## 1. Scope and time

- Settlement month: November 2025. Settlement days are the local dates 2025-11-01 through 2025-11-30.
- Local time zone: America/Chicago. Daylight saving time ends 2025-11-02 at 02:00 CDT (07:00Z); the local hour 01:00-02:00
  occurs twice. Every instant in this contract is an absolute instant; convert local wall times with the offset in force.
- Boundaries: a meter has one boundary on each local date 2025-11-01 through 2025-12-01 (31 boundaries). The boundary
  instant is the meter's scheduled read time on that boundary date: 00:00 local on the date, or, for meters scheduled at
  23:00, 23:00 local on the previous date. The schedule in force on a boundary date comes from Appendix R of the manual
  (latest effective_from on or before the boundary date) together with approved errata described in the manual.
- The 2025-11-01 boundary comes from `mdm_october_close_reads.csv` (already validated by MDM at the scheduled instant).
  The 2025-12-01 boundary belongs to the settlement; 2025-10-31 and 2025-12-02 do not.

## 2. Identifiers

Normalize every identifier before joining: apply Unicode NFKC, map Cyrillic К/А/С to Latin K/A/C, remove all
whitespace, upper-case. Meter ids are `KV` + 7 digits, account ids `AC` + 8 digits, fixture ids `FX` + 6 digits: when
fewer digits are present, left-pad the numeric part with zeros. Feeder ids are F01-F12 (`F1`, `f01`, `FDR-01` and
`F 01` all mean F01). Service points are `SP` + 9 digits (drop hyphens). Cirrus device EUIs are 16 hexadecimal digits
(upper-case, drop `:`). Legacy ids are `L-` + 6 digits (the hyphen may be missing). A read whose identifier does not resolve
to a registered meter, or that resolves only through a link that is not valid at the read time, is ignored.

Text exports are UTF-8 unless a head-end profile says otherwise. Records end with LF or with a bare CR, mixed within one
file; both terminate a record. Calendar continuation lines start with one space after the terminator.

## 3. Candidate reads

A head-end row is a candidate for meter m only if:

1. its identifier resolves to m (section 2 and the head-end profile);
2. its register value is present (profile missing-value markers are absent values);
3. after clock correction its true instant lies inside the meter's service window. Clock correction: subtract the meter's
   offset from `meter_clock_offsets.toml` when (reported instant - offset) is earlier than the meter's CLOCK_SYNC event in
   `collector_events_2025-11.log`, or when the meter has no CLOCK_SYNC event; otherwise the reported instant is already true.
   A meter removed by a completed exchange order has no reads at or after the exchange instant; the replacement meter has
   no reads before it.
4. its true instant is within 900 seconds (inclusive) of one of the meter's boundary instants. That boundary is the
   candidate's boundary. A row more than 900 seconds from every boundary instant is not a boundary read.

The manual lists the exclusions that remove candidates and the precedence that selects one value per boundary. Among
several surviving actual reads for one boundary, the one closest to the boundary instant wins; on equal distance the higher
sequence number wins. Register values are kWh; values printed with more than three decimals are rounded half-even to three.

## 4. Register arithmetic

Energy is computed per register (import, export) from an ordered list of anchors: the resolved boundaries, the final
register of a meter removed by a completed EXCHANGE order, the initial register of its replacement, the register reading
recorded by a completed CT_CHANGE order, and REGISTER_RESET events (pre-reset and post-reset values). Missing boundaries are
skipped (a gap). Walk the anchors in time order keeping the previous accepted anchor:

- A REGISTER_RESET contributes (pre_reset - previous) and the post-reset value becomes the previous value.
- Otherwise delta = value - previous. If delta < 0 and the previous value is at least 90 percent of 10^digits while the new
  value is below 10 percent of 10^digits (digits from `register_rollover.toml`), the register rolled over: add 10^digits.
  Any other negative delta at a boundary invalidates that boundary: it is skipped and the previous anchor is kept.
- Each accepted delta is a segment ending at the anchor instant. Its energy in Wh is delta x 1000 x the multiplier in force
  one second before the segment end, and it is attributed to the local date of (segment end - 1 second). A gap therefore
  places all energy since the last accepted anchor on the closing day; gaps are never interpolated. Energy after the last
  accepted anchor is not settled this month.
- Multiplier in force at an instant: the latest of (registry row valid_from at 00:00 local, completed CT_CHANGE order at its
  completion instant, CT ratio verification with action CORRECT_RATIO at 00:00 local of verified_on), on equal instants
  order beats verification beats registry. Ratios written `N:5` mean multiplier N.
- Meter accuracy: for a meter with a FAIL accuracy test dated in November (latest test date, then highest test id), each
  daily import Wh on dates before the test date becomes round_half_even(Wh x 100 / (100 + as_found_error_pct)).

## 5. Billing determinants

For meter m and settlement day d:

- Account: the registry row of m valid on d (valid_from <= d < valid_to; empty valid_to is open), then follow completed
  merges effective on or before d (predecessor to successor, repeatedly) as the manual defines.
- Vacant: the resolved account has status FINAL in the account master with final_date <= d. Disconnected: the latest
  disconnection register event dated on or before d is DISCONNECT. Energy on vacant or disconnected days, or with no
  registry account, is unbilled.
- Export credit: export Wh is credited only on billed days when the meter has an APPROVED net metering application with
  effective_from <= d < effective_to (empty = open) and the latest export inspection dated on or before d is PASS (a FAIL
  on the same date wins). Uncredited export is ignored everywhere.
- Territory of a meter day: the territory of the meter's feeder (Appendix T connectivity, manual Table F).

Unmetered lighting fixtures are billed on deemed energy. A fixture is active on d when installed <= d < removed and no
audit finding REMOVED is effective on or before d. Watts: the latest WATTAGE_CHANGE audit effective on or before d, else
the inventory nominal watts. Hours: a burn-hour exception for the fixture and date if one exists; else DUSK_DAWN uses the
feeder's dusk-to-dawn hours for the date, PART_NIGHT uses those hours less 6.00 (never below zero), ALL_DAY uses 24.00.
Hours are applied at two decimals (round half-even). Deemed Wh per fixture day = round_half_even(watts x hours). Deemed
energy is billed to the fixture's account like import, with the fixture feeder's territory.

## 6. Day types, rates and charges

Day type of (territory, d): HOLIDAY if a CONFIRMED HOLIDAY event of that territory covers d; else CPP if a CONFIRMED CPP
event covers d; else WEEKEND on Saturday and Sunday; else WEEKDAY. An event covers DTSTART <= d < DTEND. CANCELLED events
do not exist. Rate code of an account on d: as the manual defines. Base energy rate (cents per kWh) of (rate code, d, day
type): the tariff book row, replaced by an erratum where the manual says so. Rider adders: every rider in force for the rate
code on d whose applicability flag (latest effective_from on or before d for the rate code, rider and territory) is Y, plus
the storm recovery rider where the manual says so. Export credit rate: `net_metering_export_credit_rider.docx`. All rates
are applied at five decimals, rounded half-even when printed longer; follow every document note about units.

Charges per account: energy = sum over its billed import and deemed Wh of Wh x base rate / 1000; rider = the same with the
summed adders; export credit = sum of credited export Wh x credit rate / 1000. Accumulate exactly, then round each of the
three monthly sums half-even to integer cents once. export_credit_cents is the negated rounded credit.

## 7. Feeder injection and losses

Each feeder has 2884 interval-ending 15-minute intervals: (2025-11-01 00:00, 2025-12-01 00:00] local, including the repeated
hour. An interval value is invalid when its quality is not good, its value is missing, or a COMM_FAIL alarm in
`scada_alarm_journal.log` overlaps it (alarm RAISED before the interval end and CLEARED after the interval start). The value
used per interval follows the manual's SCADA precedence. Demand values are applied at two decimals of kW, energy values at
three decimals of kWh or six of MWh (round half-even when printed longer). Demand in kW over a 15-minute interval is
kW x 250 Wh, rounded half-even to whole Wh; kWh and MWh convert exactly.
Feeder injection = sum of its interval Wh. Feeder losses = round_half_even(injection x loss_pct / 100) with loss_pct from
manual Table F as amended by approved errata.

## 8. Unaccounted-for energy

System UFE Wh = total injection + total credited export - total losses - total billed import - total deemed. UFE is
allocated to accounts in proportion to their billed import + deemed Wh, excluding accounts with weight zero and accounts
exempt on 2025-11-30 (ACTIVE exemption with effective_from <= 2025-11-30 < effective_to, empty = open). Use the largest
remainder method on whole Wh: allocate floor(|UFE| x w / W), then give one more Wh to the accounts with the largest
remainders, ties to the lower account id; a negative UFE is allocated as its magnitude and negated.
UFE price (cents per kWh) = (sum of energy_cents + sum of rider_cents) x 1000 / (total billed import + total deemed Wh),
rounded half-even to six decimals. ufe_charge_cents = round_half_even(ufe_wh x price / 1000).

## 9. Deliverables

CSV files: UTF-8, no BOM, comma separated, LF line endings, one header row exactly as listed, no quoting, a final newline.
JSON files: `json.dumps(obj, indent=2, sort_keys=True)` followed by a newline. Integers are JSON integers.

- `account_settlement.csv`: account_id, rate_code, billed_import_wh, billed_export_wh, deemed_wh, energy_cents, rider_cents, export_credit_cents, ufe_wh, ufe_charge_cents, total_cents. One row per resolved account with
  non-zero billed import, credited export or deemed Wh; rate_code is the code on the account's last day with any of those;
  total_cents = energy + rider + export credit + UFE charge. Sorted by account_id.
- `meter_summary.csv`: meter_id, feeder, import_wh, billed_import_wh, unbilled_wh, export_wh, corrected_boundaries, estimated_boundaries, missing_boundaries, invalidated_boundaries. One row per meter with at least one resolved
  boundary in its service range, sorted by meter_id. import_wh is all attributed import (after the accuracy correction),
  billed_import_wh its billed part, unbilled_wh the rest, export_wh the credited export. The four counts classify the
  meter's boundaries in its service range (old meter up to its exchange day, replacement meter from the day after).
- `read_resolution.csv`: meter_id, boundary_date, source, import_kwh. One row per boundary in a meter's service
  range whose import value is not an actual read: CORRECTION, ESTIMATE, MISSING (no value) or INVALIDATED (section 4).
  import_kwh has three decimals (the rejected value for INVALIDATED, empty for MISSING). Sorted by meter_id, boundary_date.
- `interval_injection.csv`: feeder, interval_end_utc, injection_wh, source. interval_end_utc as
  YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SSZ; source is ERRATUM, RELAY_TEST, HISTORIAN or GAPFILL. Sorted by feeder, then time.
- `lighting_deemed.csv`: fixture_id, account_id, burn_days, deemed_wh. Fixtures with deemed_wh > 0; burn_days
  counts days with deemed Wh > 0. Sorted by fixture_id.
- `daytype_calendar.csv`: territory, date, daytype for TE, TN, TS, TW (that order) and each settlement day, sorted by territory, then date.
- `feeder_balance.json`: {"feeders": {F01: {...}, ...}} with injection_wh, loss_wh, billed_import_wh, billed_export_wh,
  deemed_wh, unbilled_wh, residual_wh (= injection + billed export - loss - billed import - deemed) per feeder; meter
  energy counts on the meter's connectivity feeder, deemed energy on the fixture feeder.
- `control_report.json`: {"batches": [...]} one object per head-end read file including the period 4 resend, sorted by
  file name: file, data_rows (data records counted in the file itself: reads, plus REV records in the resend), manifest_rows
  (the record count the vendor manifest reports for the file), mdm_status (from the batch control log), rejected_rows (rows the
  batch control log excludes; all rows of a REJECTED batch), withdrawn_rows (rows the vendor manifest withdraws).
- `settlement_summary.json`: account_count, meter_count, injection_wh, loss_wh, billed_import_wh, billed_export_wh,
  deemed_wh, unbilled_wh, ufe_wh, ufe_price_cents_per_kwh (string with six decimals), energy_cents, rider_cents,
  export_credit_cents, ufe_charge_cents, total_cents (sums over account_settlement).
