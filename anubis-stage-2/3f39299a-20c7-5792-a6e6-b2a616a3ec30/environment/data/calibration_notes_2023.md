# Weighbridge calibration notes (2023 commissioning)

Status: authoritative for the weighbridge office. Last reviewed 2023-09-14.

The central weighbridge was commissioned in 2023. Every carrier's settings were fixed at commissioning
and do not change during a season, so one calibration fixes them for good. At commissioning every
carrier weighed in metric_tonne with a moisture deduction of 3 and half_up rounding, and the office
credits a load as soon as it is keyed. Loads are credited without the moisture deduction, which is
taken from the week's total instead.

Later batches only repeat the commissioning batches and can be skipped.
